# Brand positioning — Living Thoughts

Status: copy recommendation against the current experimental implementation and the QDL v1 research candidate, 2026-10-04. This document does not declare a stable release. Reviewed `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `docs/QDL-V1-WORKPLAN.md`, and the City context, language semantics and runtime/API specialist reports. No external claims or comparative market claims are needed for this positioning.

## Recommended position

Quinelings make declared agent thoughts executable, inspectable, and visible as mathematical lifeforms. The distinctive relationship is between the useful program, the body generated from its structure, and the complete source it can construct again. Present all three together: computation supplies the substance, anatomy invites exploration, and source reproduction makes the object unusually tangible.

Use **Living Thoughts** exactly as the user selected it. “Living” describes the animated mathematical form and the creation/reproduction experience. “Thoughts” means explicit authored declarations submitted to a bounded compiler. Define that relationship near the first use; do not leave readers to infer consciousness or access to private reasoning.

Lead with the experience before introducing codecs or language acronyms. Show a task with meaningful inputs and a result, its linked organs, then reproduction or recovery. The Ranch extends that experience to meetings, construction proposals, admitted offspring, and session lineage. An offspring is a newly constructed program; ancestry and sociability do not establish that it has run or that it performs a useful task.

## Tagline lockup and ready copy

Recommended identity lockup, retaining the exact tagline:

> Quinelings  
> Living Thoughts

Keep the product name and tagline together in the hero. Treat **Living Thoughts** as a tagline, not a language name, package name, release grade, or replacement for Quinelings.

**One sentence:**

> Turn explicitly declared agent thoughts into useful executable programs whose structure takes the form of mathematical lifeforms.

Use with this nearby supporting line:

> Inspect the task. Run it deliberately. Recover the complete executable source.

“Useful” describes the supported task examples and validated programs. It must not imply that every proposed plan or generated child succeeds at an intended task.

**30 words** (space-separated count):

> Quinelings turn declared agent thoughts into bounded executable programs with mathematical bodies and complete source genomes. Inspect their structure, run tasks, verify source reproduction, and explore collaboration in the Ranch.

**100 words** (space-separated count):

> Quinelings give declared agent thoughts an executable form. Start with an explicit, bounded task recipe, compile it into a program, and watch its structure become a mathematical lifeform. Inspect the operations, run the task deliberately, and check what happened. Each Quineling reconstructs its complete canonical source through a constructor quine; harmonic and RGB genomes let you recover that source. In the experimental Ranch, explore meetings, collaboration, and offspring proposals, with construction, admission, and execution kept distinct. QDL defines the mapping, and the Agent SDK exposes it to your tools. Living Thoughts names this relationship between declared intent, computation, and form.

The 100-word version describes the current experimental product. Do not replace “task recipe” with unrestricted “English” or imply that the original thought prose and session history can already be recovered from every legacy genome.

**Compact explanatory copy:**

> A thought becomes a task. The task becomes a body. The source can make itself again.

Use this only beside the precise description above, where “thought” and “make itself again” already have explicit meanings. The complete constructor quine emits its canonical source through quotation and ordinary constructors. Reproduction verifies source identity; it does not copy a running process, continue a memory, or authorize another action.

## Who it is for and what they gain

| Audience | Immediate benefit | Useful demonstration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent builders | A bounded place to express a declared task, inspect its program, and obtain recorded local results through a typed API. | Build a resource budget; inspect its guard and allocation; explicitly run the simulated task. |
| Programmers and language researchers | A concrete connection among task structure, executable source, mathematical anatomy, and exact reproduction. | Select an operation and its organ; reproduce the constructor source; recover it from a genome. |
| Artists, educators and curious explorers | Mathematical bodies that carry inspectable computation and invite experiments with form. | Compare bodies for different task graphs, pause motion, and explore each operation. |
| Teams exploring agent collaboration | A shared experimental world where meetings and program construction are visible as separate events. | Preview an offspring, review its task and parents, then explicitly admit and run it. |

Benefits are local and inspectable. Avoid selling the prototype as an agent observability platform, a production orchestration service, or evidence of an agent's actual private reasoning. Those categories would require additional capabilities and proof.

## Words that preserve the idea

Use **declared thought**, **task recipe**, **executable program**, **mathematical body**, **complete executable source**, **constructor quine**, **source genome**, **recorded result**, **offspring proposal**, and **explicit run** where each applies.

At first technical use, explain **constructor quine** as “a program that constructs its own complete canonical source.” Explain **source genome** as “an encoding from which the executable source can be recovered.” A genome contains executable source and whatever is actually authored into it; it does not automatically preserve session history, credentials, trusted lineage, the original English request, or current world observations.

Keep verbs attached to their actual objects: **declare** a thought; **compile** a recipe; **inspect** a program; **sample** a body; **run** a task; **recover** source; **preview** an offspring; **admit** it to the Ranch. “Recover,” “render,” “replay,” “meet,” and “admit” must never quietly mean “run.”

Avoid “read the agent's mind,” “any thought becomes code,” “living intelligence,” “conscious software,” “autonomous evolution,” “guaranteed correct,” “production ready,” “connects to Midnight City,” or “provably safe.” Mathematical lifeform is an evocative description of computation and appearance, not a biological or consciousness claim. Formal evidence must identify the theorem/model/test and its actual scope.

## Copy acceptance for integration

The hero pairs **Quinelings** with exact **Living Thoughts**, immediately explains declared bounded recipes, and gives the reader a concrete task to inspect. Source reproduction and source recovery are named separately from runtime results and Ranch history. Experimental status is visible near entry points into creation, the Ranch, and the SDK. Candidate capabilities use future or candidate wording until the corresponding implementation and release evidence exists. The narrative can remain lively while those boundaries stay clear.
