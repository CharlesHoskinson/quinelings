Sherlock · Moriarty Execution Engine

A small computer whose internal workings are intentionally visible.

Sherlock is the execution engine at the center of Moriarty. Instead of every loan, payment arrangement, or swap inventing its own execution machinery, Sherlock gives supported programs one common machine with one common meaning. A calculator only tells you that 40 − 30 = 10; Sherlock establishes which prepared program (ProgramImage) ran, what committed state (MachineImage) it began from, which of the 39 fixed machine rules fired on each step (StepRecord), and what fuel and stack limits applied — turning “follow the agreement” into an unbroken chain that a cryptographic proof can verify without trusting the solver that ran it.

01 · Instruction book, desk & bookmarks Nothing disappears into a hidden runtime stack.

Think of the prepared program (ProgramImage) as a fixed instruction book, current values as the contents of a desk, and saved function calls as bookmarks telling the machine where to return. At every step Sherlock keeps four things in plain view: what program is running, where execution currently is, what values and unfinished work it carries, and how much execution budget (fuel) remains permitted.

02 · Reading the six split-arc rings Outermost is oldest waiting work; innermost is the active frontier.

When fact(4) calls fact(3), it cannot multiply by 4 yet. It leaves two frames on the ring: Teal on the left (Slot A) saves the caller’s variables, and Gold on the right (Slot B) holds the waiting math 4 × [?] (Amber marks operands or arguments still in progress; Violet marks an open if, match, or let; Cyan marks a let scope). Rings fill from R1 inward to R6 and clear from the inside out as answers return.

03 · The 39 silicon floor modules (A080–A225) Every step fires exactly one physical rule module on the chamber floor.

Drop inside the chip via Inside Hollow Chip and look down at the chamber floor: those 39 gold-socketed 3D silicon chiplets are Sherlock’s complete transition table (sherlock-k-a/1). On each tick, the single rule governing that step (such as A135 PushFrame, A149 RightOp, or A080 Done) physically rises from its socket and lights its status bar while the prior step’s module glows in trail mode.

04 · Why every single step is counted Arithmetic alone is not authority — the path matters.

A final answer of 10 could conceal an unauthorized recipient, an inflated solver fee, or a skipped guard. The 10-sector center dial shows the active control state, the 39-rule floor proves which rule justified the transition, and the outer rim drains 1 unit of fuel per charged step so WitnessPlan and ConstraintPlan can lock the entire trace into a Midnight proof.

01 · Delegation & Authority

Why Sherlock exists — Freedom to search vs. authority to spend

Suppose a Borrower owes a Lender 40 units under a Moriarty credit agreement that permits an external solver to execute installments of 5..40 units to Account_Lender with at most a 1-unit fee. If the solver proposes paying 30 units, a calculator can confirm that 40 − 30 = 10. But if the solver routes those 30 units to its own account (or Charlie) or claims a 3-unit fee, the subtraction is still 10 while the agreement’s authority is violated.

Sherlock separates a solver’s off-chain freedom to search from its on-ledger authority to settle. The owner’s agreement is frozen into a fingerprinted ProgramImage; Sherlock executes candidate inputs step by step under strict limits; and Midnight verifies the resulting proof without trusting the solver or exposing private witness data.

02 · State, Frames & Continuing Duties

The machinery inside Sherlock — Explicit frames & resumable state

Named functions stay fixed in the ProgramImage while the machine’s explicit state (Snapshot) advances one step at a time: current Control, immutable values (UInt128, Bool, List, Tree), lexical Environment, saved continuation frames, and remaining fuel. Entering a call saves caller bindings in Frame::Return; binary operators push Left([?] * rhs) and rewrite in-place to Right(4 * [?]) (Rule A149); Let rewrites in-place to Scope (Rule A145); and Args advances in-place (Rule A177).

In continuing agreements, Sherlock keeps four concepts distinct: finishing a computation (Done), completing an economic obligation (0 owed), saving a resumable agreement (packing 30 paid and 10 remaining into a committed MachineImage), and splitting proof work into chunks (ReferenceSlice). A failed tentative attempt in Stage 2 is discarded off-ledger without erasing Stage 1’s committed history.

03 · Spatial Hardware Mapping

How the 3D chip maps to the abstract machine

Central 10-Sector Dial & Outer Fuel Rim: The center stator lights the active Control state of sherlock-k-a/1 (EVAL, PUSH, RET, INVOKE, ENTER, BOUND, CASES, MATCH, PRIM, ERR) and aims its transfer needle West (Slot A) or East (Slot B) at the newest visible frame, while the 48-segment outer rim drains 1 unit of fuel per charged rule.

Six Concentric Split-Arc Rings (R1..R6) & Open Bore: Ordered chronologically from oldest waiting work at the perimeter (R1.A = frames[len − 1]) to the active frontier on the inside (frames[0]), each ring pairs two 180° half-arcs: Teal / Sky-Cyan for saved caller and let environments, Amber / Gold for pending operands (4 × [?]), and Violet for if, match, and let gates.

04 · Inside the Hollow Vault

The 39-rule decoder floor & the unbroken StepRecord chain

Switch the camera to Inside Hollow Chip or Cross-Section to inspect the 6 × 7 matrix of 39 physical 3D silicon rule modules (A080..A225) on the vault floor (y = −3.38). Instead of hiding interpreter logic in host software, sherlock-k-a/1 defines every legal transition as one of these 39 elementary rules across six categories.

On every tick, Sherlock emits a StepRecord and the justifying floor module physically rises out of its gold socket and lights its south-lip beacon, while the previous step’s module retains a decaying trail glow. That visible handoff represents the exact mathematical chain where Step[i].after == Step[i+1].before: the WitnessPlan arranges the private StepRecord values for the prover, and the ConstraintPlan enforces the fixed algebraic equations of the active rule.

When you enter Inside Chip (drag down or scroll down the vertical elevator) or view Cutaway, the floor of the chamber exposes 39 gold-bezeled 3D silicon rule modules arranged in 6 columns by 7 rows (with the SHERLOCK · K-A/1 · 39-RULE ROM plaque in the lower-right corner). Together, these 39 modules form Sherlock’s entire instruction set: every single transition in a recursive calculation or financial agreement must fire exactly one module from these six categories:

1. Terminal 0F 3 rules · Charge 0F · Indices 0–2

Free publication rules that convert a completed or halted control state into a public outcome without spending fuel.

A135 PushFrame A140 FrameErr (E4)
4. Continuation Frame 1F 10 rules · Charge 1F · Indices 15–24

Handles Control::Return(v) meeting the top waiting frame: rewrites frames in-place (A145, A149, A177) or pops them to branch or compute.
