From thought to program
Start with a task: “Find a route around the closed road.” Express it in QDL as typed operations and their connections. The interpreter reads the supplied observations, runs each operation when its inputs are ready, and records the result.
QDL makes the task explicit: what it observes, what it computes and what action it proposes. You can inspect the program and every recorded step.
From program to Quineling
The source also describes an anatomy made from mathematical curves. Operations are assigned regions, colors group their roles, and connections show how values flow. The renderer animates this anatomy; the interpreter runs the task when you press Run.
