# Final experimental QDL: a lifeform design bible

## Judgment from the present collection

I inspected `research/chroma-collection.png`, the existing reference analysis, current design documentation, and all ten program descriptions. The collection now has meaningful hues and distinguishable mathematical families. Its main weaknesses are that membranes often read as thin wire illustrations, many portraits have equal-weight strands throughout, and the upright Lanternkeeper/Raincatcher/Seedbank group shares a similarly narrow, unresolved center. Threadsorter currently resembles two disconnected glasses lenses; it needs a central body and wing roots. The goal is an unmistakable living silhouette with a coherent mass, not maximal filament complexity.

A deterministic body-only measurement of the current screenshot is in `research/final-qdl-lifeforms-measurements.json`. It finds bright coverage of 14.7% of the Wayfinder bounding box, 15.7% for Swarmwarden, and 17.7% for Threadsorter, compared with 32.3% for Lanternkeeper. Those are RGB threshold diagnostics rather than a perceptual proof, but they support a focused examination of why some bodies read as sparse line drawings. Do not simply brighten every sample: consolidate a few structural ridges and give one quieter membrane a visible continuous mass.

## Three priorities

1. **A body before detail.** Each species needs one dominant mass, one secondary mass or continuation, and a restrained tertiary fringe. Build a visible central body for the moth, a coherent leading mass for comet, and an interior chamber for torus. Detail should reveal the body rather than compete with it.
2. **A distinctive signature gesture.** Every species has a principal posture and a recognizable preparation/stroke/recovery. Motion belongs to the entire organism; root attachments and colored territories remain coherent. Permit stillness and asymmetry. One signature gesture matters more than three added oscillators.
3. **Semantic anatomy that survives beauty.** Preserve stable operation ownership, dependencies, source identity, and recorded-value states under every deformation. Use actual program structure to shape the organism; use inspection and trace overlays to reveal precision without putting a diagram across its portrait.

## Shared rules

- The background stays quiet and almost black. Keep a generous clear margin and avoid bright competing UI inside the specimen field.
- Use one luminous anatomical center and approximately 2–5 long dominant crests. The exact number is a visual heuristic, not a new validator bound. Recessed tissue and distal fringe are visibly quieter.
- Think of material as translucent tissue: a visible colored midtone membrane, sparse depth marks, long colored ridges, and a narrow pearl core. Do not let additive overlap bleach all role color into white. Shape can be beautiful in Pearl study before hue is added.
- Role hue means the same thing across species. Individuality comes from silhouette, posture, curvature, spacing, tissue thickness, gesture, and attachment pattern. Do not recolor an action cyan merely to match a species mood.
- A portrait has an anatomically plausible attachment story, even when abstract. A root, chamber, neck, rim, or hinge joins parts. A strand that floats near a mass does not feel attached.
- Major surfaces move together. Secondary detail follows. Keep large changes slower and fine ripples smaller; never run all amplitudes at their maxima simultaneously.
- A still frame is a complete recognizable specimen. Reduced motion chooses that specimen frame rather than an arbitrary collapsed phase.
- Treat mathematical periodicity, visible circulation, and task execution as separate concepts. A moving ring may be tissue circulation; a bounded repeat remains an explicit program annotation.

## What belongs in QDL and what belongs in the view

Keep authored vocabulary small. Most of this update can be achieved by improving the existing family formulas/presets and using existing composition, surface, light and rhythm controls. Do not add ten species-specific records or arbitrary shader code.

If a new record is necessary, add one optional bounded **gesture** declaration: fixed gesture enum, strength, and normalized bounded cadence; the detailed poses remain closed implementation primitives. Gesture belongs in canonical source because it characterizes the intended creature. Existing composition focus/lean/yaw/pitch and material controls already specify posture and tissue presentation. Further independent fields such as 'intelligence', 'emotion', 'health', 'confidence' or 'awareness' are unjustified unless tied to a real, precisely named program quantity; omit them.

Presentation-only choices: current phase, pause, selected specimen, active role/lens/Pearl study, current recorded task occurrence, current trace replay position, camera zoom/inspection, and temporary selection emphasis. They cannot modify source, dispatch a task, or create receipts. Camera/selection cannot change scalar domain or classify a missing value as zero. Improving material lighting globally does not require storing transient lighting results in each program.

Authoring edits change canonical identity; rendering choices do not. Improved family default appearance should remain documented as experimental renderer behavior, without freezing a language version or promising archived pixel equivalence.

## The precise translation story

There are four different layers to keep legible:

1. **Source** fixes task program plus authored QDL.
2. **Structural anatomy** deterministically projects operation roles, stable IDs, level, degree and declared dependencies into anchors, tissue territories and pinned connections. The silhouette is intentionally a lossy projection.
3. **Living presentation** deforms the anatomical material using the bounded gesture and independent view phase. It adds no computation graph node, graph edge, evidence, or action.
4. **Recorded outcome** supplies the explicitly selected trace/lens values and an optional execution replay. Source mismatch becomes stale. A result projection is a declared presentation of a recorded output, not a newly invented internal operation.

A person should be able to click a colored region and see its real owner and the inputs that shape it. One human-readable example for each specimen is more useful than a paragraph claiming it embodies abstract thought.

## Acceptance tests

- At one chosen reduced-motion frame and at four representative phases, all ten species retain their distinctive primary masses. Compare in grayscale, Pearl study, role colors, and at 64/96/160 CSS pixel heights. Ask at least two viewers to match unlabeled thumbnails to the ten species after seeing a labeled sheet; target 8/10 or better, investigate confusion rather than treating an automated shape metric as human recognition.
- Moth wing roots and jelly tentacles visibly meet their body/rim at every sampled phase; seeded root and coral root do not drift. Mathematical endpoint coincidence tests supplement videos.
- Lanternkeeper, Raincatcher and Seedbank remain different at thumbnail size: tall sentinel, broad folded sheet, compact kernel with crown. Color alone cannot satisfy this test.
- A ten-second film shows each principal gesture clearly enough to describe in one sentence. No arbitrary per-frame noise, abrupt seam jump, floating attachment, camera pumping, or flickering material owner.
- Lens tests cover zero, missing, invalid, stale, underflow and overflow without fabricated values. Role hues remain stable under motion. No preview, pause, trace replay or lens switch increases task executions or action receipts.
- Alter executable node/edge structure and confirm anatomy changes consistently. Alter only presentation phase and confirm canonical source, graph, scalar values and quine identity do not change. Reproduction preserves authored gesture if introduced.
- Confirm the same species reads on dark mobile displays without making every line brighter. Benchmark after any lighting or geometry change; use a fixed vertex budget and prefer caching over additional particle layers.

The supplied reference's lesson is hierarchical density and coherent phase, not a requirement that every species imitate its upright figure. The original analysis in `docs/DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md` already separates the artist's figure from our design interpretation. This bible gives each Quineling its own body and choreography while retaining the shared mathematical and semantic grammar.
