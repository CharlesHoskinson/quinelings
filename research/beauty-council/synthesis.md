# Beauty council: the homepage falls short

All twelve reviewers answer **no** for equivalent beauty on the current homepage and **partly** for the archived gallery. Three GPT reviewers, three Gemini 3.1 Pro High reviewers, three Grok 4.7 xhigh reviewers and three Opus 5.5 High reviewers examined the original and the two Quinelings presentations separately. [Provenance and evidence limits](README.md) describe exact model confirmation and capture coverage.

The original combines a bright braided core, translucent overlapping filaments, irregular negative space and sparse trailing dots. Its sampled poses change the outline substantially. The homepage repeats a chamber with two arms and a curled stem; its color bands and small joint changes carry most of the variation. The archived gallery has stronger folds, luminous lines and distinct families, but its regular modules and fairly even light still fall short of the reference.

These are subjective judgments, not a human preference study. Scores differ between reviewers, especially on variety. The original is one artwork; the library contains ten. Filmstrips establish pose differences but do not measure smooth playback, frame pacing or biological life. Some reviewers omitted gallery motion rather than inventing evidence.

## What to improve

1. **Form and variety.** Author connected folded ribbons, asymmetric branching and tapering filaments with larger negative spaces. Preserve operation-to-tissue ownership and task behavior. Each new design becomes a new source; a copied reference drawing is a useful study, not an executable translation.
2. **Light and texture.** Build luminous density from many faint marks, with bright crossings, translucent intermediate strands and sparse peripheral dots. Use normals and depth in the viewer, preserve the role-color assignments and keep the exact RGB genome separate. The homepage currently unions marks per owner into one filled path, losing overlap accumulation inside that path.
3. **Motion.** Author phase-coupled local deformation and traveling folds, with a larger pose range and coherent return. Camera movement alone does not demonstrate body deformation. New gestures must carry their own source identity and retain source recovery and bounded sampling.
4. **Composition.** Fit the complete pose envelope with one consistent camera and use a near-black specimen ground. Compare before/after at common phases and camera bounds. Larger framing can reveal detail; it cannot create a new silhouette.

## Convergence and acceptance

Start with one authored program whose body can stand beside the reference. Establish its contour, density hierarchy and motion before generalizing the grammar to ten distinct families. Then map branching, data flow and authored constants into those forms. Preserve a legible path from the task to each operation, its tissue and its recorded value.

Acceptance should include side-by-side phase captures and actual playback, explicit source/design identities, equal task outputs where only the body changes, exact emitted/recovered source, an ownership overlay that still points to the evaluated operation, keyboard/reduced-motion behavior and a measured rendering budget. Re-run the council on the new artwork. Do not certify equivalence from more particles, a passing interpreter test or a revised description.

## Proposals excluded from direct implementation

A few Gemini proposals describe editing the retained `anatomy.js` sampler while preserving the AST. That changes the geometry interpretation and does not preserve frozen behavior. Use newly authored designs or an explicit visual profile instead. The original reconstruction is a two-dimensional sampler with perceived depth, not evidence of a three-dimensional physical field. Blended white light also cannot serve as the exact opcode color or source byte strand. These corrections constrain the next aesthetic implementation.

The functional audit loop closes separately: [52 prepared runtime cases and the implementation checklist](../website-overhaul/implementation-checklist.md) establish what the programs actually calculate and recover. The beauty gap remains a concrete design problem.
