What makes it a Quineling?

A Quineling can also produce the complete written program needed to reconstruct a copy of itself.

Why the name “Quineling”?

Its source is the complete written program: the instructions and information needed to reconstruct it. A quine is a program that outputs its own complete source. Quinelings combine that ability with a mathematical body whose regions are assigned to the program’s steps.

The body is built from the connections between steps and an authored design. The same program and design produce the same body. New readings change its recorded values while keeping that body. This creature follows its written instructions; it does not interpret a request in English on its own.
