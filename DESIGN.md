# Quinelings website

The user chose a creature-led introduction, guided lesson, and field guide. Impeccable directs the redesign's layout, hierarchy, typography, accessibility and bounded visual review.

## Visual world
A living mathematical exhibition with the reading discipline of a field guide. The artifact leads; editorial prose explains its mechanism beside it. Ink-dark aubergine grounds the emitted light of the creatures. Warm paper text, pale jade actions and restrained ochre annotations keep body colors legible. Self-hosted EB Garamond supplies the display voice; a system sans supports controls and sustained reading; monospace is reserved for source and numeric data.

## Structure
Shared conventional navigation: Start here, Explore, Create, Run & inspect, Ranch, Field guide, Quinelings in Midnight.City, The mathematics of Quinelings, Developers. The last two thematic sections begin as placeholders. The homepage teaches one water calculation: predict the total, run, inspect a step and its body region, then change the readings and run again. It introduces terms at the point of use. Full comparisons, source edits, retries, offspring and reconstruction live in the separate laboratory. The field guide supplies optional explanations and reference; developers use the SDK guides. Preserve these distinct reader goals. No process reports or artificial marketing metrics. Research and model learner reviews guide the sequence; they do not establish measured learning gains.

## Components and states
Open editorial sections separated by space and hairline rules. No decorative kickers, icons or nested cards. Selected controls have visible border and color changes, plus aria state. Focus rings, selection and disabled/loading/error states belong to the same palette. Mathematical motion is the signature; surrounding text remains still.

## Responsive and motion
Desktop pairs task and body; narrower screens stack them without hiding controls. Tables scroll in their own region. Body drawings retain finite source-authored samples and semantic ownership. Hidden or offscreen bodies stop redraws; reduced-motion visitors begin paused. Sampling and labels reuse existing geometry wherever possible without changing source.
